Who We Are

Logistics That Move You Forward

TruWays Logistics (Pty) Ltd is a privately owned logistics company established to provide exceptional logistics solutions.

We are committed to delivering customised and efficient solutions that help our customers move forward, while maintaining high standards of service, safety and compliance.

TruWays is a 100% black-owned business with B-BBEE Level 1 accreditation.
